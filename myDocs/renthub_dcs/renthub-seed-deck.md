## 💔 1. The Problem: The Nigerian "Agent Tax"

In Nigeria's rapidly urbanizing centers, renting a home requires surviving an unregulated, predatory cartel of street middlemen.

* **Tollgate Viewing Fees:** Tenants pay ₦5,000 to ₦15,000 upfront just to look at an apartment.
* **The 10%–20% Extortion:** Unaccredited street agents demand ₦200,000–₦600,000 for 15 minutes of unlocking a gate.
* **Double-Letting Scams:** Rogue agents collect cash from 5+ different victims for the same unit and vanish.
* **Hostage Caution Deposits:** 10%–20% caution deposits are pocketed immediately and virtually never refunded upon lease end.
* **Annual Renewal Shakedowns:** Agents demand fresh 10% commissions every 12 months for doing nothing.

## 🛡️ 3. The Solution: The 7-Stage Trust Chain

RentHub replaces chaotic street negotiations with a bank-grade, verified transaction workflow:

```
[1. Discovery] ──> [2. Free Inspection] ──> [3. Application] ──> [4. Landlord Accept]
                                                                        │
[7. Review] <──── [6. Key OTP Release] <── [5. 100% Escrow Pay] <───────┘
```

1. **Discovery & Verification:** Properties audited for light meters, borehole water, and title deeds.
2. **Zero-Fee Calendar Tours:** Direct booking with landlords; zero viewing fees permitted.
3. **Formal Application:** Pre-screened tenant profiles with algorithmic TrustScores.
4. **48-Hour Offer Lock:** Listing locked upon landlord acceptance to prevent multi-letting.
5. **100% Escrow Deposit:** Funds held in a segregated collection account with a licensed banking partner, separate from RentHub operating funds.
6. **Key Handover OTP Release:** Secret 6-digit code exchanged only after physical inspection.
7. **Verified Multi-Dimensional Reviews:** Authentic ratings on condition, utilities, and conduct.

## 🔐 4. The 6-Digit Key Handover OTP Protocol

**The Uncompromising Core Rule:** *Never release money without keys. Never surrender keys without the code.*

```
Tenant deposits rent into Escrow ──> Segregated Collection Account holds funds
                                                    │
                                     System generates secret 6-digit OTP
                                                    │
Tenant inspects water, power, doors ──> Tenant hands OTP to Landlord in exchange for keys
                                                    │
Landlord submits OTP in Dashboard ────> Programmatic 98% Net Rent Transfer to Landlord Bank
```

* **Custody Structure:** Tenant payments are collected into a segregated account held with a licensed banking partner and released programmatically to the landlord via bank transfer APIs only upon OTP confirmation.
* **48-Hour Dispute Window:** If a property has undisclosed major defects, the tenant reports an issue, escrow freezes, and RentHub arbitrates within 48 hours.
* **Handover Safeguard:** If a tenant takes physical possession but withholds the OTP without filing a dispute within 48 hours, the landlord submits handover evidence (meter handover/keys) to trigger administrative review and disbursement.

## 🔄 7. The Moat: Defending Against Disintermediation

**The #1 Question:** *"Why won't landlords and tenants renew off-platform in Year 2 to skip the 4% fee?"*

RentHub creates **four structural lock-ins** that make staying on-platform the better choice for both sides:

1. **Rent Relief-Ready Records:** Under the *Nigeria Tax Act 2025* (effective January 2026), employees can claim a deduction of 20% of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000, with documentary evidence. On ₦2M rent that is a ₦400,000 deduction from taxable income. RentHub auto-generates the lease, receipts, and payment trail in one claim-ready record.
2. **Protected Caution Deposit:** The caution deposit stays in the segregated account until move-out and is settled against a condition report. Tenants get evidence-based refunds; landlords get damage claims backed by funded deposits. Going offline forfeits both protections.
3. **Portable Tenant Track Record:** Tenants build an authenticated rental history (TrustScore) that earns priority viewings, lower deposit requests, and access to future partner-provided rent financing, with no credit risk on RentHub's balance sheet.
4. **Landlord Operating Tools:** One-click renewal proposals, TrustScore-vetted applicants, automated agent commission settlement, and digital receipts put a landlord's whole lease workflow on RentHub.

<div class="footnote-box">
  Renewal assumptions in our model: 75% of expiring leases renew on-platform in Year 2 (300 of 400) and 60% in Year 3 (1,500 of 2,500). Rent relief per the Nigeria Tax Act 2025; rent payments must be documented (receipts, transfer records, or lease).
</div>

## 🤝 8. The Regulated Agent Model (Managed Supply)

Rather than fighting the existing broker ecosystem, RentHub turns verified agents into regulated field partners for busy and diaspora landlords.

### Fixed Policy Rules (Decided & Enforced in Code):
* **Landlord-Paid Only (Capped at 5%):** Tenants **NEVER** pay agent fees. Landlords can voluntarily delegate listing management at an agreed commission capped at **5%** (deducted from their gross rent upon escrow release).
* **Zero Side-Payment Rule:** Agents are strictly prohibited from demanding viewing fees, gate money, or off-platform cash.
* **The 3-Strike Enforcement System:**
  * Tenants can file one-click reports with evidence if an agent solicits off-platform cash.
  * Strikes operate on a **90-day rolling expiration**.
  * **3 upheld strikes = Permanent platform ban and revocation of all listings.**
